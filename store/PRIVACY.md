# Privacy Policy — Pack It All

_Last updated: November 2026_

**Pack It All** ("the game") is a casual game. This policy explains what data is used and why.

## What we do NOT do
- No accounts or sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## Game progress
Your coins, levels, city and settings are saved **only on your device**. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Optional ads (Google AdMob)
The game never shows ads on its own. An ad plays **only when you tap a "watch ad" button** to get a reward (for example a free heart or double coins).

To show these ads the game uses **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and process:
- the device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking on iOS),
- approximate location derived from the IP address,
- basic device and app information and ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or completed).

This data is used to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you have agreed, personalise them. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

- **Europe / UK:** you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and can change your choice any time from **Settings → Privacy options** in the game.
- **iPhone:** iOS asks you whether to allow tracking. If you say no, you can still watch ads; they will just be less personalised.
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
