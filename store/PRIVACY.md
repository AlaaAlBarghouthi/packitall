# Privacy Policy — Pack It All

_Last updated: November 2026_

**Pack It All** ("the game") does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

- **No accounts, no sign-in.** The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any other personal data.
- **Progress is saved only on your device.** Your coins, levels, city and settings are stored locally on your phone and never leave it. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
- **No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.** The game contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- **No internet required.** The game works fully offline.
- **Children.** The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

If a future version adds features that use data (for example in-app purchases or ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
