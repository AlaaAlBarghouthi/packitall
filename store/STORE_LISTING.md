## Full description — English
Drag the magic hole around colorful 3D worlds and swallow everything in your path!
Every item you swallow makes your hole bigger and gives you coins. Complete each level's goals
before the timer runs out, then spend your coins to build and upgrade your very own city.

★ Satisfying swallow gameplay – start small, grow huge, swallow teddy bears, cakes, cars and buses!
★ 5 worlds: Beauty, Sweets, Toys, Picnic and City Streets – endless levels.
★ Boosters: Magnet, Tornado, Freeze time and Grow.
★ Build your city: cafés, ice-cream shops, fountains, a cinema, a hotel, a Ferris wheel, a castle and more.
★ Every building earns coins while you're away – upgrade them to earn even more.
★ Daily tasks and free gifts.
★ Plays offline. Arabic & English.
